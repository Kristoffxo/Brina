    How it works
    1. 1

      Open a chat

      No signup, no name, no phone number. You start typing and the chat is open.

    2. 2

      Type whatever you want

      For as long as you want. No time limit, no subject you have to stick to.

    3. 3

      End it whenever

      Press end and delete and the whole conversation is erased. Not archived — deleted.

    What this is, and what it isn’t

    What it is

    • A person who reads what you write and answers.
    • Free, with nothing to pay for later.
    • No judgement about what you bring up.
    • No advice unless you ask for it.
    • Anonymous — no account, no name, no number.

    What it isn’t

    • Therapy or counselling.
    • Medical or crisis support.
    • A diagnosis, or an opinion on medication.
    • Run by anyone with clinical training.
    • Open 24×7 — not yet.
    Questions
    Is it really free?

    Yes. No fee, no subscription, and no paid version waiting behind this one.

    Who am I talking to?

    Right now, one person — whoever started this, based in Prayagraj. Trained volunteers will join later, and this page will say so when they do.

    Is it anonymous?

    Yes. You don’t give a name, an email, or a phone number, and you are never asked who you are.

    Are chats saved?

    Only while the conversation is open, so the messages can reach the other person. They’re deleted the moment either of you ends it.

    Can I talk about anything?

    Mostly, yes — work, family, a breakup, a bad week, or nothing in particular. If you say you’re thinking about ending your life, the answer will point you to a trained helpline, because that is the right help and this isn’t it.

    Are you a therapist?

    No. There is no clinical training, licence, or medical supervision behind this, and nothing said here is treatment or advice about your health.

    What if nobody is there when I write?

    The dot at the top says whether anyone is around, and you can write either way. Your message waits, and gets read and answered when someone is back.

    Do I have to be 18?

    Yes, this is for adults only. If you tell us you are under 18 the conversation will be stopped and you’ll be pointed to services meant for young people.

    Listening, as a volunteer

    We’re training a small group of listeners in Prayagraj. The training happens in person, over a few sessions. You need to verify your identity with a government ID, and you have to be 18 or over.
