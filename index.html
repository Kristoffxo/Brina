How it works

  1. 1

    Open a chat

    No signup, no name, no phone number. You click the button and the chat is open.

  2. 2

    Type whatever you want

    For as long as you want. There is no time limit and no subject you have to stick to.

  3. 3

    Close it whenever

    Shut the tab, or press end and delete, and the whole conversation is erased. Not archived — deleted.

You don’t need a reason

Most people freeze at the first message. If that’s you, borrow one of these and go from there.

  • Today was long and I don’t really know why.
  • I moved here for work and I haven’t spoken to anyone all week.
  • I keep replaying something I said and I can’t put it down.
  • Nothing happened. I just don’t want to sit alone right now.

Hindi, English, or a mix of both — whichever comes easier. There is no right way to write here.

What this is, and what it isn’t

What it is

  • A person who reads what you write and answers.
  • Free, with nothing to pay for later.
  • No judgement about what you bring up.
  • No advice unless you ask for it.
  • Anonymous — no account, no name, no number.

What it isn’t

  • Therapy or counselling.
  • Medical or crisis support.
  • A diagnosis, or an opinion on medication.
  • Run by anyone with clinical training.
  • Open 24×7 — not yet.

If you’re in crisis

If you are thinking about ending your life, or you are in danger right now, please talk to people who are trained for it. They answer at any hour and the calls are free.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no fee, no subscription, and no paid version waiting behind this one.

Who am I talking to?

Right now, one person — the person who started this, based in Prayagraj. Trained volunteers will join later, and this page will say so when they do.

Is it anonymous?

Yes. You don’t give a name, an email, or a phone number, and you are never asked who you are.

Are chats saved?

Only while it is open. Your messages sit on a server so they can reach the other person, and they are deleted the moment either of you ends the conversation — anything left idle is deleted within a day, automatically.

Can I talk about anything?

Mostly, yes — work, family, a breakup, a bad week, or nothing in particular. If you say you are thinking about ending your life, the answer will be to point you to a trained helpline, because that is the right help and this isn’t it.

What if nobody is there when I write?

The pill near the top of the page says whether anyone is around, and you can write either way. Your message waits, and gets read and answered when someone is back.

Are you a therapist?

No. There is no clinical training, licence, or medical supervision behind this, and nothing said here is treatment or advice about your health.

Do I have to be 18?

Yes, this is for adults only. If you tell us you are under 18 the conversation will be stopped and you’ll be pointed to services meant for young people.

In crisis? Call 14416 — free, 24×7.
